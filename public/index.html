    What the colours mean

    • working — it is doing something right now
    • thinking — it is deciding what to do next
    • walking in — it just arrived at a desk
    • needs you — it stopped and is waiting for an answer
    • finished — it wrote its report
    • on a break — no news for a while, so it wandered to the cooler

    Sprinting means it did three or more things in four seconds. The bar under each name shows how far through its own plan it is. An agent that never wrote a plan has no percentage, so its bar keeps moving instead of guessing.
